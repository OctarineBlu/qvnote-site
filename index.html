How it works

Three steps. One of them is talking.

  1. 1

    Hold and speak

    One big button. Hold it, say what happened in your own words — meals, medicines, symptoms, readings, activity — and let go. A quiet voice is fine.

  2. 2

    Check the cards

    Every word it heard is shown, with a card for each thing it found. Fix a word, split a card, or tell it what something is — it remembers for next time.

  3. 3

    Save

    Nothing is stored until you tap Save. Then it sits on your timeline at the time it happened — “yesterday at eight” lands on yesterday at eight.

Privacy

Built on privacy first.
Nothing leaves your phone.

Not the recordings, not the words, not the records. QVeeNote has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere — with or without a connection.

  • Speech recognised on the device

    The recogniser runs inside the app. It is not the phone maker’s dictation and there is no server.

  • No account, no cloud, no tracking

    Nothing to sign up for. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers.

  • Backups are yours

    Make an encrypted backup when you choose and keep it where you like. Only your passphrase opens it.

What it gives you

Say it once. Keep it for good.

Every sentence you speak becomes a record you can read back, compare and share. Nothing to type, nothing to file, nothing forgotten.

It is your memory, with the dates filled in. Which day the headache started, what you ate before it, when you last changed the dose — it is all there, in your own words, at the time it happened.

Medicines

“Took my morning meds.” Four ticks.

Call a medicine what you call it — “the blue inhaler”, “my BP tablet” — and group the ones you take together. One sentence logs the lot; untick anything you skipped. A dose that differs from usual is flagged, not assumed. Did I take it? When? The question answers itself.

Trends

Does what you eat, or do, change how you feel?

Put a symptom or a reading beside anything else you log — a food, an activity, a medicine, your sleep — over 30 days or more, and see the days line up, or not. Headaches beside strawberries. Blood pressure beside the evening walk. It shows what you logged, side by side; it never tells you why. That conversation is for you and your doctor.

Summary report

See the big picture. Share it if you choose.

Pick a period and QVeeNote writes a clean PDF: every medicine with how often it was taken, every reading with its low, high and last, the symptoms you tracked, and a space for the questions you want to raise. Read it yourself to see the month at a glance, or bring it to an appointment so the conversation starts from what happened rather than what you can remember. It leaves your phone only when you share the file.
