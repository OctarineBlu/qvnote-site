Support

QVeeNote is in early access. We read every message.

Something isn't working

Email support@qvnote.com with your phone model and what you said to the app. If a recording was misheard, the quickest way to help is to send it from the entry's page (turn on “Help improve QVeeNote” under More › Diagnostics, open the entry, and choose “Send this recording”). The file holds that one recording, the words as heard and the entry as it stands — nothing else.

Privacy questions

privacy@qvnote.com — see the privacy policy first; the short version is that nothing leaves your phone unless you send it.

Early access

QVeeNote is in closed beta on Google Play. The three steps to join are on the beta page. If you'd rather ask first, write to hello@qvnote.com and tell us which phone you use, which country you're in, and how you heard about QVeeNote.
