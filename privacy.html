Privacy policy

Effective 4 November 2026.

QVeeNote does not collect, transmit or share your data. Everything you record stays on your phone.

What the app records

QVeeNote keeps what you record on your phone, and nowhere else:

  • what you say to it (a voice recording and its transcript), and the entries it makes from that: medicines, symptoms, readings such as blood pressure or weight, food, activity and notes;
  • the names you teach it for medicines, symptoms, foods and activities, and the settings you choose;
  • a name for the Summary report, if you type one.

You can delete any of this at any time: single entries, all voice recordings, or everything. Voice recordings are deleted automatically after 90 days unless you choose to keep them.

What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you do it yourself. QVeeNote has no internet permission: it cannot connect to any server, and there is no account. Data leaves the phone only when you:

  • create a backup — an encrypted file protected by a password you choose, saved where you choose;
  • create a Summary report — a PDF you choose to share, for example with your doctor;
  • send a recording — you can choose to send one voice recording with its corrected words, to help us improve speech recognition. It goes only through the app you pick, and your name is not in it;
  • send a diagnostic file — only if you turn on “Help improve QVeeNote” in Settings, and only when you choose to share the file. It contains timings and counts, never the words you said or the names of your medicines.

We never see any of these files unless you send them to us.

Speech recognition

QVeeNote brings its own speech recogniser and runs it on your phone. It does not use the phone’s built-in voice service, which on many phones sends audio to a server. Recognition never sends anything anywhere: no audio and no text leave the phone for it, with or without a connection. A recording leaves only if you send it yourself, as described above.

Permissions

QVeeNote asks for the microphone (to hear you) and, if you set reminders, for notifications. It does not ask for internet access, location, contacts, or Android's health data. You can check this at any time in Android Settings › Apps › QVeeNote › Permissions.

Children

QVeeNote is made for adults. It collects no information from anyone, so it collects none from children, and it is not aimed at them.

Not medical advice

QVeeNote is a personal diary. It shows what you recorded and does not interpret it, diagnose, or recommend any treatment. Do not change any medication without talking to your doctor or pharmacist.

Your rights

Your data is on your phone and nowhere else, so you already hold every right a privacy law gives you: you can see all of it, correct it, take a copy (a backup) and erase it (Delete everything), without asking us. If you write to us about this policy we will reply within 30 days. In the EU and UK you may also contact your data protection authority. In California and Washington State the CCPA and the My Health My Data Act apply; because QVeeNote collects nothing, the controls above are how those rights are met.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new effective date, and the app's About screen links to it. We will not add data collection to QVeeNote without changing this policy first and saying so in the app.
