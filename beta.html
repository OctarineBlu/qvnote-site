Join the beta

QVeeNote is in closed beta on Google Play for Android phones. Three steps, about five minutes.

1. Ask to join the testers group

Open groups.google.com/g/qveenote and choose Ask to join group, signed in with the Google account your phone's Play Store uses. In the note, tell us which phone you have, which country you're in, and how you heard about QVeeNote. We approve requests by hand, usually the same day.

2. Become a tester

Once you're in the group, open play.google.com/apps/testing/com.arboreal.qveenote on your phone and tap Become a tester. If the page says the app isn't available, your group request hasn't been approved yet, or you're signed in with a different Google account.

3. Install from Google Play

The same page then offers Download it on Google Play. Install it like any other app. Updates arrive through Play automatically for as long as you're a tester.

What to expect

The beta is currently open in Ireland, the United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand, Canada and the United States. Everything you log stays on your phone — see the privacy policy. If something is misheard or goes wrong, the quickest way to help is from the entry's page in the app (“Help improve QVeeNote”), or email hello@qvnote.com.

To leave the beta, open the tester page above and choose Leave the programme; your records stay on your phone.
